Places catalog sources

Catalog places identify their contributing source. Supported inputs include Overture Maps Foundation, reviewed City of Boulder trailheads mirrored on Kaggle, and National Park Service park identities and visitor arrival data. Each result identifies its source release and, when available, the upstream evidence date. Dataset identity is not a live confirmation of access, opening hours or availability. Attribute ratings are model estimates, not source reviews.

RoadtripsAreFun filters United States records, maps road-trip categories, normalizes coordinates and address fields, and omits unused properties. Source identifiers and attribution are retained. Coverage is limited to the areas and categories in the active import; it is not exhaustive.

Reviewed heritage-unit arrivals also use the NPS public unit inventory for identity and designation, with current visitor-facility state, coordinates, parking and access evidence kept separately. Cartographic park-label geometry is not an arrival source. Some dated driving-arrival reviews use the OSRM routing engine and © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Those network checks do not replace the original publisher's facility coordinates.
  • Harvestly Markets, derived from USDA Local Food Portal data: U.S. Farmers Markets by State 2026, Kaggle version 4, reconciled with the publisher's October 11, 2026 snapshot and current public visitor records. CC BY 4.0; source and methodology. Roadtrips filters, classifies and normalizes factual records while preserving native identities and coordinates. Historical update dates and separately attributed source seasons remain in provenance. A current listing does not guarantee a market is open on a trip date. No endorsement is implied; source data carries no warranty. Descriptions, personal contact details and photographs are omitted.
  • Ride in Park: Theme Parks of the World, Kaggle version 1, CC BY 4.0. Original Wikidata identities and coordinates are preserved; Wikidata structured data uses CC0 1.0. Roadtrips filters and classifies independently reviewed destinations, retaining operator access restrictions separately. No endorsement or warranty is implied. Operator prose, images and Wikipedia article text are omitted. Driving evidence uses OSRM and © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); a road snap does not establish parking or unrestricted access.

Only sources present in a record's retained provenance apply to that record. Overture processing provenance accompanies the upstream source; it does not replace the upstream license. Overture-signals operating-status processing uses CDLA Permissive 2.0 and requires an independently identified admitted upstream source. No Tripadvisor content is used to seed the catalog. OpenStreetMap place records are not included in this catalog version; its routing-network evidence is credited separately above.
