Places catalog sources

Catalog places identify their contributing source. Supported inputs include Overture Maps Foundation, reviewed City of Boulder trailheads mirrored on Kaggle, and National Park Service park identities and visitor arrival data. Each result identifies its source release and, when available, the upstream evidence date. Dataset identity is not a live confirmation of access, opening hours or availability. Attribute ratings are labeled model estimates or deterministic category inferences, with unknown attributes retained as uninformative priors; they are not source reviews.

RoadtripsAreFun filters United States records, maps road-trip categories, normalizes coordinates and address fields, and omits unused properties. Source identifiers and attribution are retained. Coverage is limited to the areas and categories in the active import; it is not exhaustive.

  • Ride in Park: Theme Parks of the World, Kaggle version 1, CC BY 4.0. Original Wikidata identities and coordinates are preserved; Wikidata structured data uses CC0 1.0. Operator prose, images and Wikipedia article text are omitted. Independent arrival checks retain attributed public-road and parking evidence from OpenStreetMap; native place coordinates are preserved. Roadtrips' OSM evidence and derived access assessments remain available under ODbL 1.0 in the selected arrival evidence file. Driving evidence uses OSRM and © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); a road snap does not establish parking or unrestricted access.

Only sources present in a record's retained provenance apply to that record. Overture processing provenance accompanies the upstream source; it does not replace the upstream license. Overture-signals operating-status processing uses CDLA Permissive 2.0 and requires an independently identified admitted upstream source. No Tripadvisor content is used to seed the catalog. OpenStreetMap place records are not included in this catalog version; its routing-network evidence is credited separately above.
