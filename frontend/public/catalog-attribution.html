Places catalog sources

Catalog places come from Overture Maps Foundation. Each result identifies its source release and, when available, the upstream evidence date. Dataset identity is not a live confirmation of access, opening hours or availability. Attribute ratings are model estimates, not source reviews.

RoadtripsAreFun filters United States records, maps road-trip categories, normalizes coordinates and address fields, and omits unused properties. Source identifiers and attribution are retained. Coverage is limited to the areas and categories in the active import; it is not exhaustive.

Only sources present in a record's retained provenance apply to that record. Overture processing provenance accompanies the upstream source; it does not replace the upstream license. No Tripadvisor content is used to seed the catalog. OpenStreetMap and other supplements are not included in this catalog version.
