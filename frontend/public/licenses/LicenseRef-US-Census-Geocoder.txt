Census Geocoding Services calculate positions along MAF/TIGER address ranges.
These interpolated positions are independent sanity checks of published venue
addresses, not surveyed entrances, parking locations or guarantees of access.
Native Kaggle/USDA market coordinates are preserved, never replaced by Census
points. Positional accuracy, current operation and suitability for a particular
trip are not guaranteed. Unknown addresses or conflicting successful matches
remain explicit and can prevent source admission.
