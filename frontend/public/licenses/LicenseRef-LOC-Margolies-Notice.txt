LOC's published statement explains that it purchased photograph intellectual-
property rights with the archive and reports no known copyright restrictions
on those photographs. It separately warns that depicted artwork, privacy and
publicity rights can still apply. This notice is not a blanket CC0 declaration.

This catalog uses factual metadata references and independent current visitor
and licensed coordinate evidence. It imports no photographs, depicted artwork,
marketing descriptions or reviews. Kaggle compiler Heads or Tails labels the
metadata compilation CC0. Original source identities and attribution are retained.
