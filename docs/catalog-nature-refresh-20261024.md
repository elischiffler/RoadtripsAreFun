# October 24 nature coverage and rating audit

The LA–San Diego bell corridor previously contained nine farmers markets and one
amusement park. It contained no dedicated nature destination. Its weak nature
selection is therefore an input-coverage problem as well as a rating problem.
The existing study remains historical evidence; replacement results must be
reported separately because both catalog coverage and the rating rubric change.

## Reviewed additions

Three distinct destinations are admitted through a pinned offline adapter:

| Destination component | Native OSM arrival node | Official destination facts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bolsa Chica, south trail access | 699596608 | [CDFW](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Lands/Places-to-Visit/Bolsa-Chica-ER) |
| Upper Newport Bay, Big Canyon trail access | 12512732477 | [CDFW](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Lands/Places-to-Visit/Upper-Newport-Bay-ER) |
| Batiquitos Lagoon, Gabbiano trail access | 49300796 | [CDFW](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Lands/Places-to-Visit/Batiquitos-Lagoon-ER) |

CDFW public-access GIS identities corroborate the official visitor pages. Native
OSM road nodes remain unchanged and are checked against public parking geometry;
all three actual OSRM destination responses report zero-metre snap distance.
Separate centers, beaches, boat launches and restricted fishing areas are excluded
from the descriptions. There is one record per reserve, not one per parking lot.
The full original responses and receipts remain in the private portable import.
The checked-in review records their URLs, acquisition times and SHA-256 hashes.
CDFW factual information is public-domain under its retained notice; OSM data is
credited to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL 1.0. Photographs are not imported.

The [2026–27 agency regulations](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=203137&inline)
provide sunrise-to-sunset ordinary visitor access (§550, page 61), with additional
06:00–20:00 restrictions at Bolsa Chica (§630, page 112). The adapter calculates
30 dated windows beginning October 24 using Astral 3.2 and America/Los_Angeles,
rounding opening upward and closing downward to minutes and intersecting the
fixed restriction. These are derived astronomical clocks, not published clock
observations; terrain and weather are not modeled. There is no weekly fallback.
The admission also expires after 30 days. Posted closures always govern visits;
this research does not evaluate itinerary schedules or full-trip feasibility.

The import preserves all 608 parent rows and 189 calendars, adding three rows and
three calendar companions. It uses existing tables and atomic generation switching.
No saved trips or user-account tables are modified. The source replay validates
original bytes, current-review bindings, identity, arrival, access and rights;
changed review content requires a new explicit source/code review.

## Rating correction

`attraction-traits-v3-destination-rubric` rates the exact destination component on
an absolute scale. Nearby coastal scenery is not automatically nature at a market;
walking between stalls is not hiking. Missing support does not become a 0.5 prior.
All 14 fields remain estimates generated by the configured AI gateway, with actual
model identity and timestamps persisted. The rubric version changes cache keys;
older ratings remain recoverable and are not silently overwritten.

The first ten-place audit found Leucadia Farmers Market's nature utility declining
from 0.45 to 0.06, and Solana Beach Farmers Market from 0.39 to 0.06. Those changes
are AI estimates under the revised instructions, not independent human ground
truth. All eligible inputs across the replacement study use the same revised
version. Solver settings, corridor widths, seeds and paper sweeps remain unchanged.
